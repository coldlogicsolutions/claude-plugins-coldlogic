# Manual follow-ups

Anything Derek has to do by hand (a secret to add, a setting to change, a decision to make) goes in a GitHub issue on the project's repo, assigned to derekjw and opened as the App (Decided 2026-10-26):

    gh-app issue create --assignee derekjw --title "<what to do>" --body "<why, and the exact steps>"

Say in the session that you opened it, with its link. Don't leave follow-ups only in chat, a TODO comment or a local file.
